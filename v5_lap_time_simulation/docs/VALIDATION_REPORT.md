# Retrospective 2024 descriptive comparison

## Procedure and limits

A fixed generic vehicle is used for every attempt. Fit one effective power scalar
to the pooled median official average speed of 206 complete 2020–2023 attempts:
**230.8435 mph**. Other vehicle and track parameters are assumptions. Compare
that same output with 54 complete 2024 attempts. Completeness requires four
complete canonical lap rows and finite positive official average speed and total
time. Four simulated laps are compared with the official four-lap elapsed time.
There is no car-, driver-, setup-, weather- or attempt-specific prediction.

2024 outcomes are excluded from scalar fitting, but were inspected during model
development. Therefore this is a retrospective year-separated comparison, not a
pristine prospective holdout. Aggregate agreement does not identify or validate
unique vehicle physics.

## Constant-speed benchmarks

Generated by the same run in `output/constant_baselines_2024.csv`. Mean and median
use only the same 2020–2023 training attempts. The 230 mph benchmark is a stated
illustrative fixed reference, with no claim that it was preregistered.

Physics speed bias is −0.755722 mph; four-lap duration bias is +0.504810 s.
The physics model provides **no aggregate predictive improvement over the
training-median predictor**. This equality follows directly from its calibration
objective; numerical differences are floating-point noise. Beating the mean and
230 mph references is attributable to the chosen median target, not demonstrated
physics skill. The simulated spatial profile and parameter experiments are added
engineering functionality whose spatial accuracy has not been validated here.

## No telemetry validation

Lap and section averages are not continuous speed traces. The report-supported
nine-section summaries for 2020–2023 and different 17-section set for 2024 have
no verified distance crosswalk to these eight modeled segments. No unsupported
section-by-section score is reported. Modeled segment times stay separate from
historical records. Tire, aero, line, setup and driver behavior cannot be uniquely
recovered from these aggregate observations.
